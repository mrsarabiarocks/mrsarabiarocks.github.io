About me

Teacher first. Creator second.

I'm Michaela Arabia, a middle school teacher in Southern California, and I'm still in the classroom every single day. I teach ELA and American History to grades 6 through 8, so everything I post has been tested on real twelve and thirteen year olds before it ever hits your feed.

My content lives where teaching meets technology. Blacklight Poe annotations, project based learning, Canva everything, and the AI tools I actually use to plan, grade, and get my weekends back. Teachers save my posts and come back to them when they plan, which is exactly where your product wants to be.

When I partner with a brand, it sounds like me, because my audience trusts me to tell them what works in a real classroom.
